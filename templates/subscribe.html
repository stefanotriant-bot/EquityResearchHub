Common questions

Why pay when Yahoo Finance is free?

Free websites usually show you a number but make it hard to find where it came from. Equity Research Hub takes you straight to the actual document the company filed with the SEC. Paid plans also show you 8-K filings, which is where companies announce things like lawsuits and accounting changes, plus what professional analysts think. Other paid sites charge a lot more for the same info.

Can I cancel?

Yes. One click in your Account settings. If you pay monthly, you keep access until the end of the month you paid for. If you pay yearly, you keep access for the full year you paid for.

What's the lifetime offer?

It's a one-time price you only see the first time you visit this page. If you take it, you get our top plan (Patron) for life, plus a founder badge, for a lot less than paying every month. Once you leave this page, the offer is gone and we won't bring it back.

Is this investment advice?

No. This is a research tool. We pull together public information from the SEC and other places so you can do your own homework. Any choice to buy or sell is yours, and we suggest talking to a licensed financial advisor before you invest in anything.
