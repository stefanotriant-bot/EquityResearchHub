The dividend lifecycle, plain English

Declaration date
The day the company's board announces the next dividend (amount, ex-date, record date, pay date). Usually delivered through a press release or 8-K filing.
Ex-dividend date
The first day a stock trades without the right to the next dividend. To collect this payment you must own the stock by the close of the day before the ex-date.
Record date
The day the company checks its shareholder roster. Usually one business day after the ex-dividend date.
Pay date
The day the cash actually shows up in your brokerage account. Typically two to four weeks after the record date.

Dividend data sourced from Yahoo Finance. Declaration dates aren't reliably available through this feed, check the company's investor relations page for the official announcement.
